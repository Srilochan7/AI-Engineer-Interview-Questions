# 🧠 AI Engineer / AI Internship Interview Questions
This repository contains interview questions that were asked to me for an **AI Engineer role / AI Internship** (6+ interviews). The questions are divided into different categories: Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Core AI, Generative AI, LLMs, Agentic AI, and Others.

also check out - https://github.com/Srilochan7/AI-roadmap

---

## 📌 Categories

### 1. Machine Learning
- What is overfitting?
- What is underfitting?
- What is the difference between classification and regression?
- Explain precision and recall.
- What are the evaluation techniques of ML models?
- Explain NLP text processing in detail under the hood.

---

### 2. Deep Learning
- Explain and what is the difference between RNNs, LSTM RNNs, and Transformers in detail?
- Explain gradient descent under the hood.
- What are activation functions in detail?
- What happens inside embeddings?

---

### 3. Core AI
- What are transformers? *(most frequently asked question)*
- Why are transformers better?
- Explain transformers taking an example in detail.
- Explain normalization, layer normalization, and their differences.

---

### 4. Generative AI
- What is RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)?
- What is prompt engineering?
- What is the difference between RAG and prompt engineering?
- What is the difference between fine-tuning and RAG?
- Explain vector databases.
- How to fine-tune a RAG pipeline such that it gives us the best results possible?
- If RAG is bad, what are the steps you take?
- Explain embeddings.
- Explain hybrid search.
- Explain AI evaluation techniques.
- Why are embeddings formed in vectors and plotted and not stored in DBs?
- After storing in vector DB, it extracts the data and gives it to LLM, so will it give embeddings or after converted?
- Explain multimodal generative AI.
- What is Whisper?

---

### 5. LLMs (Large Language Models)
- Explain PEFT (Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning).
- Explain LoRA and QLoRA.
- What is the difference between LoRA and QLoRA?
- Explain evaluation techniques of LLMs.
- What are the drawbacks of LLMs and how to solve them?
- What are the problems faced while fine-tuning?
- Explain the process of fine-tuning an LLM using LoRA.
- Explain the process of fine-tuning an LLM using QLoRA.
- What are the other methods of fine-tuning than LoRA and QLoRA?

---

### 6. Agentic AI
- What is LangGraph?
- Explain CrewAI and its difference from LangChain and LangGraph.

---

### 7. Others
- Explain Python OOPs.
- Is Python single or multi-threaded?
- Explain AI and LLMs for non-tech people.
